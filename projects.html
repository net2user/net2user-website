What Is This, In Plain Terms

Say you're weighing a new AI initiative before it goes anywhere near a budget conversation. You have a rough sense it could be valuable, but you also know there are risks, unknowns, and questions someone on the leadership team is eventually going to ask. The Advisor is built for exactly that moment, before you commit, not after.

What It Does

Describe the opportunity you're considering, or pick one of eight ready made scenarios, and the Advisor walks it through the same questions an experienced executive team would eventually have to work through anyway: how much value is really on the table, what could go wrong, how complex the build actually is, whether your organization and your data are genuinely ready. It hands back a clear executive briefing, a funding recommendation, and the conditions attached to it.

How It Helps You

Instead of a slide deck built once and argued over for weeks, you get a consistent evaluation you can run on every opportunity that crosses your desk, and compare them side by side. If there's something specific you want factored in that the preset scenarios don't cover, you write your own use case description and the assessment adapts to what you actually asked.

How an Assessment Works

Single Use Case Mode

Run one initiative through the full evaluation and get back an Executive Briefing, a funding recommendation such as Fund with Conditions, along with the top conditions attached to that funding and the key tension leadership needs to resolve before moving forward.

Agent Scorecards

Every assessment scores Value, Risk, Complexity, Adoption, and Data Readiness independently, each out of 100, each with a supporting figure, estimated dollar range, weeks to pilot, confidence level, so the rating isn't a black box number.

Portfolio View

Rank every use case at once across a shared scoring model, sorted into Quick Win, Strategic Bet, Long Term Play, or Reconsider, with portfolio level observations that surface patterns a single use case view would miss.

Per Agent Detail

Expand into the reasoning behind each score, Value agent, Risk and Governance agent, Architecture agent, Adoption agent, and Data Readiness agent, each with its own detailed breakdown behind the summary number.

The Thinking Behind It

The platform systematically works through the decision dimensions that actually determine whether an AI initiative succeeds, business value, AI and data readiness, implementation feasibility, governance, risk, and strategic alignment, folding all of them into every assessment. In effect, it asks the same questions an experienced executive team, an outside consultant, or a transformation office would eventually have to answer before approving a significant initiative, just structured, consistent, and fast enough to run against eight initiatives in the time it usually takes to argue about one.
